About

Meet Them in Music is a resource for music educators who want to better serve students with disabilities. Founded by Dr. P. Eric Bottorff, an elementary music educator and district content leader in Michigan, this site bridges the gap between music education and special education with research-informed, practitioner-friendly strategies.
