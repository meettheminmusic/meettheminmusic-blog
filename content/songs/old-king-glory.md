**<u>Activity</u>**

Students sit in a circle.  The "chooser" marches or walks around the circle while the song is sung.  When the lyrics say "the first one, the second one, the third one" the chooser points at the nearest students as if to count them.  

The third student follows the original student around the circle for the next repetition.  The most recent student to join their line is the "chooser."
