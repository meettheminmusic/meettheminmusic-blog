{{< callout title="Aurally Received" >}}
I learned this song aurally and have not seen in notated elsewhere.  It feels "music learning theory." <br>Though notated in c minor it may be better in e or f minor for childrens' voices.
{{< /callout >}}

**<u>Activity</u>**

Provide students with scarfs to flow around their bodies as you sing the song.  Try high, low, on different parts of the body.  

Change words for high and low.
