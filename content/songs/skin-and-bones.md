<u>**Activity:</u>**

<u>**Social Extension:</u>**

Ask the students "Why was it her job to clean up the bones?"  Talk about "Integrity" and how "doing the right thing is always the right thing to do." 

 If you see something wrong, the right thing to do is to try to fix it ... Maybe not cleaning up dead bodies, though!
